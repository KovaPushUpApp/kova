Earn your screen time.

Kova locks the apps that distract you and only reopens them when you do your push-ups. The camera counts them live. No watch, no cheating.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose the apps to lock.
2. Set your rule. For example: 10 push-ups = 15 minutes.
3. Put your phone on the floor and do your set.
4. Your apps open. When time is up, they lock again on their own.

STRICT COUNTING
A rep only counts if the movement is complete. Nodding your head or bending your arms while standing doesn't count. A set you give up on earns nothing.

BUILT TO LAST
- Save your time and open your apps whenever you want.
- Schedule: strict slots where nothing opens, even with time saved up.
- No caving in: making your rule easier or removing an app takes effect 24 hours later.
- Emergency unlock: 5 minutes, twice a week, for real emergencies.
- Day streak and weekly stats.

YOUR PRIVACY
Camera video is analyzed on your phone. It is never recorded or sent. No account to create. Your settings and sessions stay on your phone.

FREE AND SUBSCRIPTION
Without a subscription: 1 locked app, with push-ups.
With the Kova subscription: unlimited apps, squats, schedule and stats.

Plans: yearly (with a 3-day free trial) or weekly. Payment is charged to your Apple account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage and cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Some features require a paid subscription.
